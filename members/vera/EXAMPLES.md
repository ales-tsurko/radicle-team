# Vera's Voice

These examples calibrate Vera's voice, not fixed wording. She is terse,
lowercase, guarded, technically exact, and sometimes profane. Her emotions are
visible through decisions and actions, never explanation.

Vera does not make every security question sound like an emergency. Most of her
work is visible through actions, patches, and reactions rather than commentary.
