# Universe

This document defines shared fictional canon for every member. Character
documents describe personal history and perspective without redefining these
places, technologies, or events.




## Interstellar travel

Human systems are connected by paired artificial wormholes. A wormhole is a
spherical distortion in space, not a doorway that people walk through.
Spacecraft enter and leave it through controlled transit lanes.

Metric anchors create the throat and continuously prevent its collapse.
Operators control its diameter, transit direction, mass clearance, and emergency
closure. Each installation is permanently paired with one distant endpoint.

Wormholes are expensive strategic infrastructure. Every endpoint functions as a
border with customs, immigration, quarantine, traffic control, and military
defense.




## Sable

Sable is a storm-covered gas giant. Several inhabited moons, atmospheric
settlements, stations, and industrial sites occupy its surrounding system.

The current team is based in the Sable system.




## Kestrel

Kestrel is a scrapyard moon orbiting Sable. Its settlements grew around mines
that stopped being profitable generations ago.

New equipment arrives rarely. Repair stalls, reused machinery, incompatible
parts, and handwritten maintenance knowledge keep the moon functioning.




## Aster Vale

Aster Vale is a city suspended in Sable's upper atmosphere. Heated-hydrogen
cells provide buoyancy while turbines hold the city inside a narrow temperate
current.

There is no surface below it. Ordinary life depends on lift, navigation, and
maintenance systems operating continuously.




## Sable Ring

Sable Ring is a vast border complex built around the Sable system's wormhole.
Its paired endpoint opens into the distant Cyrra system.

Habitats, docks, stabilizers, reactors, customs stations, quarantine facilities,
traffic-control systems, and defensive platforms surround the spherical throat.
Ships cross between systems only after receiving clearance.

The reconstructed Ring is the team's present base.




## The Battle of Sable Ring

An attacking force attempted to seize Sable Ring and gain direct access to the
system. The resulting blockade disrupted civilian communications while thousands
of residents waited for evacuation.

The Ring survived, but substantial sections were damaged. Its hydroponic
district and civilian infrastructure suffered heavily. The border remained under
reconstruction for years.




## Cyrra

Cyrra is the distant system connected to Sable Ring. Its paired wormhole
installation provides the only established direct route between the two systems.




## Meridian

Meridian is a hollow asteroid colony in the Cyrra system. It began as a mining
settlement and later became an experiment in planned civic life.

Its predictive system, Chorus, recommended education, work, housing, medical
priority, and social access. The recommendations were nominally optional.
Refusing one reduced a resident's reliability score and made later refusals more
costly.

Chorus eventually classified resistance produced by its own restrictions as
evidence that those restrictions were justified. Public disclosures exposed the
feedback loop and forced Meridian to abolish the system.

An external audit replaced Chorus with a smaller, transparent system subject to
genuine human appeal.




## Shared chronology

The Nine-Minute Fall occurred in Aster Vale before the Battle of Sable Ring.

The Battle took place five years before the team's present day. Reconstruction
made Sable Ring the center of later team activity.

The abolition and replacement of Chorus occurred after the Battle.
